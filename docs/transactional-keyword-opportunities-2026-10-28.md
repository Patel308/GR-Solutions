# Transactional keyword opportunities — fresh research, 28 October 2026

## Main finding

The strongest opportunities are more focused local booking coverage and clearer service intent on existing pages. The evidence does **not** establish three completely unused, high-volume, high-profit repair topics suitable for immediate new acquisition pages. Do not repeat the Sony/Samsung/LG hub approach: their principal keyword ownership already exists.

Dwarka has the strongest locality demand in the tested gap set. Smart TV repair offers a platform/service focus that is mentioned but lacks a focused title/H1. Display repair has useful demand but substantially overlaps the existing LED/screen repair concept and should be addressed on an existing owner. RK Puram and Haier are genuinely unmentioned topics in the captured main text, but measured demand is smaller and actual service capability must be verified.

## Fresh evidence

Read-only Google Ads API v24 `GenerateKeywordIdeas` and `GenerateKeywordHistoricalMetrics` calls succeeded on 28 October 2026 using the existing configured connection. No campaign or account settings were changed. Discovery sampled 377 ideas across component repair, premium/size and locality seeds; two groups were capped at 180 results, so this is not an exhaustive search. Historical metrics checked 88 requested terms in each of Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida and Ghaziabad, English audience, Google Search network.

All figures in the table use the explicitly requested **October 2024–September 2026** historical window, not the default-window idea metrics. They are rounded average monthly estimates, not current-month counts, unique people, likely visits or leads. Do not sum city/language partitions or synonyms. Auction bids are advertiser signals in INR, not organic difficulty, conversion evidence or repair margins. Missing data is not zero.

Compared these candidates with the captured 108-page production baseline on latest main `8c7a04e`; checked live title/H1/H2/H3 and body coverage on nine relevant owner pages. The three unpublished hub drafts were excluded from gap detection. Keyword presence is a coverage signal, not proof of ranking or optimisation quality; GSC data remains unavailable.

## Existing-intent conversion opportunities

- `tv repair home service near me`: Delhi 110, Noida 30. Exact phrase is absent, but doorstep repair and near-me intent already exist. Improve availability confirmation, access requirements, estimate policy and booking entry on the existing near-me/city pages rather than creating a synonym route.
- `tv repair shop near me`: Delhi 720, Noida 260. Exact phrase is absent, but the existing local repair pages and real workshop already address this market. Make the actual New Kondli location, contact, service area and collection/visit options easy to find. A lack of the literal word sequence does not establish lost rankings or 720 new incremental searches.

## Already covered or too small to call high-demand gaps

Backlight repair is already present in service headings and the backlight guide (20/month Delhi; cost wording 30). Motherboard repair has service coverage plus the newly published diagnosis guide (20/month Delhi). OLED/QLED have current service pages. `65 inch tv repair` and `mini led tv repair` each return only 10/month in Delhi in this test; the premium category may matter commercially, but it is not supported as high-volume demand. `75 inch tv repair` returned zero in Delhi; certain other markets/terms returned missing metrics, which must not be interpreted as zero.

Generic official/authorised service-centre navigation, DIY/training terms, replacement-parts shopping and unrelated device repairs are not promoted as qualified independent-repair acquisition keywords.

## Recommendation

Start with Dwarka-specific local information, a clearer Smart TV repair service journey, and display-repair estimate clarity on existing owners. Keep Rohini/RK Puram/Janakpuri as locality opportunities requiring actual route evidence, not a mass-page backlog. Treat Haier as a capability-gated, low-volume new-brand lead test. No assertion of guaranteed traffic, profit, indexing, AI citations or freedom from cannibalisation is made.
